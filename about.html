About Me

My Journey & Aspirations

Hello! My name is Sanjana Gowda, and I am a high school junior at Emerald High School in Dublin, California. Ever since I began coding, I have been deeply fascinated by how technology and engineering can address real-world problems. Whether designing simple applications or implementing advanced models, I love using technical skills to build meaningful solutions.

Beyond writing code, a significant focus of my efforts is dedicated to making STEM fields more inclusive. As a co-president of Girls Who Code, I work to establish supportive spaces for girls who want to learn computer science, helping them build the confidence to explore engineering pathways. I aim to continue developing my expertise in computer science and research through hand-on internships and project-based collaborations.

Core Principles

"I believe that the best technical applications are those built with direct empathy for the community they serve. Technology is a tool to empower people, improve accessibility, and drive positive progress."
